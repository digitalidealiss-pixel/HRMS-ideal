# IDEAL HRMS — Administrator & HR Manual

## Roles
- **SUPER_ADMIN**: Full system access including Settings, Shift Policies, Role Assignment, Audit Logs, and Deployment Kit.
- **HR_ADMIN**: Onboarding, Employee Directory, Attendance Monitoring & Corrections, Leave Approvals, Payroll Processing, Performance Appraisals, Tasks, and Reports.

## Daily HR Operations
1. **Onboarding**: Use *Employees > Onboard Employee*. Employee IDs (`IISS-XXXX`) and annual leave balances are provisioned automatically.
2. **Attendance Monitoring**: Review daily check-ins, selfies, GPS coordinates, and pending correction requests under *Attendance*.
3. **Field Monitoring**: Open *Live GPS* to view active field employees on the interactive map and inspect daily movement trails.
4. **Payroll Processing**: Configure per-employee salary structures and statutory toggles (PF, ESI, PT, TDS) under *Payroll*, then run monthly generation. Review `DRAFT` amounts before marking `APPROVED` and `PAID`.
5. **Audit & Compliance**: Super Admins can inspect all system mutations in *Settings & Audit*.
