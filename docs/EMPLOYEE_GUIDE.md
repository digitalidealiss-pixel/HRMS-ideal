# IDEAL HRMS — Employee Usage Guide

## Accessing the HRMS
1. Open the company HRMS web URL in **Google Chrome** (Android/Desktop) or **Safari** (iPhone/iPad).
2. Tap **Install App** (or Safari *Share > Add to Home Screen*) to add IDEAL HRMS as a mobile PWA. No Play Store or App Store download is needed.

## Daily Attendance
1. Open **Attendance** from the bottom mobile navigation bar.
2. Tap **Open Front Camera** to capture your attendance selfie, and allow **Location Permission** when prompted.
3. Tap **CHECK IN** at shift start and **CHECK OUT** at shift end.
4. If your internet is disconnected in a field site, your attendance is stored safely on your phone and marked **Pending Sync (Device-Captured)**. It synchronizes automatically when signal returns.

## Live Location Sharing (Field Staff)
1. Open **Live GPS** and tap **Start Location Sharing** during authorized field hours.
2. Tap **Stop Location Sharing** at the end of field duty.

## Leaves, Payslips & Tasks
- View remaining leave balances and apply for full or half-day leave under **Leaves**.
- Download official PDF salary statements under **Payslips** once approved by HR.
- Update task progress sliders and completion notes under **Tasks**.
